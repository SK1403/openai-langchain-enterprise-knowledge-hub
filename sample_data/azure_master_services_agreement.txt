MICROSOFT AZURE ENTERPRISE CLOUD AGREEMENT (ECA)
Agreement Identifier: MSFT-AZURE-ECA-2025-001
Effective Date: January 15, 2025
Parties: Contoso Global Enterprises ("Customer") and Microsoft Corporation ("Licensor")

SECTION 1: AZURE OPENAI & DATABRICKS SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS (SLA)
Licensor guarantees a monthly uptime percentage of at least 99.9% for Azure OpenAI API endpoints and 99.95% for Azure Databricks workspace clusters deployed with Multi-AZ redundancy.
In the event of an SLA breach, Customer shall receive a 25% Service Credit against the applicable monthly billing cycle.

SECTION 2: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Except for liability arising from gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of Section 4 (Confidentiality & Data Protection), neither party's aggregate liability under this agreement shall exceed the total monetary amounts paid by Customer under this agreement during the twelve (12) months preceding the incident.
Consequential, punitive, indirect, or lost profit damages are expressly disclaimed by both parties.

SECTION 3: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & DATA PRIVACY
Customer retains sole and exclusive ownership of all Customer Data, proprietary prompts, embeddings, and fine-tuned model artifacts.
Azure OpenAI Service Commitment: Customer Data, inputs, and outputs are NOT used to train or improve any Microsoft or OpenAI base models. Data remains strictly within the designated Azure geography (East US 2 / West Europe).

SECTION 4: TERMINATION AND MIGRATION
Customer may terminate this Enterprise Agreement for convenience upon ninety (90) days prior written notice.
Upon termination, Licensor must provide full export capabilities of all Delta Lake tables and ADLS Gen2 storage blobs without penalty fees.
